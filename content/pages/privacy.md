## 1. TL;DR
I don’t collect any data.

## 2. Nothing Gets Collected
I don’t ask for your name, email, address, phone number, or anything else.
I don’t use cookies, analytics, or any tracking tools.

## 3. No Ads or Trackers
There are no ads, social-media buttons, or hidden scripts that send data to other companies.
You just browse, and that’s it!

## 4. External Links
If you click a link to another site, that site may have their own rules.
You'd probably want to read their privacy info before sharing anything.

## 5. Kids Under 13
I don’t knowingly collect data from children under 13.
If you think I have something about your child, please let me know so I can delete it.

## 6. Changes to this Policy
If I ever decide to start collecting data, I’ll update this page.
Always check the date at the top!

## 7. Questions?
Feel free to email me at [hello@shinjustin.com](mailto:hello+dotcom@shinjustin.com).
I promise to get back to you as soon as I can.
